Felix Webhook Relay is starting. The tokens Deployment writes the broker
credential to the Secret {{ include "relay.brokerSecret" . }}; on a first
install, enable the Felix brokers with it:

  helm upgrade <felix release> <felix chart> --reuse-values \
    --set broker.enabled=true \
    --set broker.credential.existingSecret={{ include "relay.brokerSecret" . }}

Senders post to the Service {{ include "relay.fullname" . }}-intake at /in/<tenant>/<source>,
and admins sign in on the Service {{ include "relay.fullname" . }}-admin.
